I guide wilderness canoe trips at Camp Wanapitei, taking youth on 6&ndash;30 day
expeditions in Northern Ontario and Quebec. My longest trip so far was 54 days,
self-sustained, from the Yukon into the Northwest Territories.

The rest of the year I kayak whitewater on the Ottawa and Gatineau, and I've
spent two summers planting trees in northern British Columbia.
