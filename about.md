I'm a PhD student in Astronomy at the Center for Astrophysics &#124; Harvard &
Smithsonian, where I work on fast radio bursts and what they can tell us about
the baryon content of the universe.

I did my undergraduate degree in Honours Physics at McGill, splitting my time
between the Dark Matter Group and the Cosmic Dawn Group &mdash; which is roughly
how I ended up interested in both hidden-sector particles and the statistics of
large cosmological surveys at the same time.
