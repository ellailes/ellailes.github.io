Ella Iles

PhD student in Astronomy · Center for Astrophysics | Harvard & Smithsonian

I work at the interface of cosmology and particle physics — using astrophysical systems as laboratories for physics beyond the Standard Model, and developing statistical tools for the next generation of line-intensity-mapping and 21 cm surveys.

My current work is on fast radio burst cosmology: what a large sample of localised FRBs, and the dispersion measures they carry, can tell us about the distribution of baryons in the intergalactic medium.

Before Harvard I read Honours Physics at McGill, where I worked with Katelin Schutz on dark matter phenomenology and with Adrian Liu on cosmological survey statistics.
